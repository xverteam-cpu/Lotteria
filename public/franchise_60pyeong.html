Verification Checklist & Next Steps

Before presenting any amount as final, confirm every major cost line with official documents.

Franchise Agreement

Confirm franchise fee, package inclusions, territory rules, royalties, and brand obligations.

Supplier Quotations

Secure kitchen equipment, signage, POS, furniture, and opening inventory quotes.

Lease & Landlord Terms

Review rent advance, deposit, construction bond, design rules, utilities, and turnover condition.

Construction Bid

Validate fit-out through plans, engineering scope, material specs, and contractor pricing.

LGU Compliance

Confirm business permits, sanitary, fire safety, occupancy, and city-specific requirements.
